# Project plan and system design — template

Copy this file into your group repository as `docs/plan.md`, fill it in, and hand in the PDF export by the deadline. Keep it short: two to four pages. The plan is presented at the plan seminar and is the basis for opposition round 1.

## 1. The challenge and your interpretation
One paragraph: which industry challenge, and what you will actually build — the narrower and clearer, the better.

## 2. System design
A block diagram of the system (components, data flows, interfaces) and a few sentences per component. State where the Period 1 toolbox is reused and where the agent will generate new code.

## 3. Work breakdown structure
Number the work packages. For each: what is delivered, who is responsible, and which other packages it depends on.

| WP | Deliverable | Responsible | Depends on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ... | ... | — |

## 4. Time plan
A simple GANTT: work packages against the five project weeks (w43–w50, with the freeze on 11 Dec as the hard right edge). Mark the three opposition tags and the fishbowl week.

## 5. Resource and role plan
Who does what: driver/reviewer rotation, who curates the decision log, who owns the evaluation harness, who prepares each opposition round.

## 6. Risks
The three most likely ways this project fails, and what you will do about each — one line per risk. "The agent produces code we cannot verify" is a legitimate and expected entry.
